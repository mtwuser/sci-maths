## 1. Progressive `M1 -> M4` Mathematical Mastery Ladder

Each learner stage builds from geometric intuition to rigorous axiomatic proof and physical/computational application:

| Mastery Stage | Cognitive Goal & Socratic Focus | Core Mathematical Pillars | Verification Gate (`verify-mastery`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`M1` (Foundations)** | Geometric intuition before symbolism; visualize transformations and rates of change. | Linear systems, $2\text{D}/3\text{D}$ vectors, single-variable derivatives & integrals, first-order ODEs. | Solve boundary cases (`N vs N±1`) and explain *why* each step holds without memorizing formulas blindly. |
| **`M2` (University Rigor)** | Formal $\varepsilon$-$\delta$ and linear-algebraic proof construction; connects directly to Classical Mechanics & Electromagnetism (`sci-physics`). | Inner-product spaces, Spectral Theorem (`MATH-01`), Multivariable & Vector Calculus (`MATH-02`), Sturm-Liouville ODEs/PDEs (`MATH-03`). | Construct complete deductive proofs (`Hypothesis -> Stepwise Deduction -> Q.E.D.`) and verify dimensional/domain constraints. |
| **`M3` (Applied / Systems)** | Numerical stability, conditioning ($\kappa(A)$), and variational/symplectic methods. | PDE discretization, Fourier/Laplace transforms, variational calculus (Euler-Lagrange). | Analyze stability thresholds and conditioning cut-points. |
| **`M4` (Frontier Mastery)** | Functional analysis, differential geometry, and operator algebras. | Unbounded operators on Hilbert spaces, Lie groups/algebras, tensor calculus on manifolds. | Synthesize cross-branch theorems with `citation_receipt` provenance (`E3`). |
